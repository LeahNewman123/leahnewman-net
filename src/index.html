Projects

Wi-Fi Controlled Robotic Buggy

January–April 2026

I developed Arduino firmware and a Processing interface for wireless control and live monitoring. The buggy integrated motor control, encoder feedback, ultrasonic obstacle sensing and infrared line sensors. I implemented and evaluated PID speed-profile tracking using recorded speed data and error measurements.

ScopeLab

July–August 2025

I co-founded ScopeLab to enable wireless, real-time monitoring of robotics data and system health. I led numerical, computational and mechanical development, including signal decoding, ASCII conversion, data display and CAD models for the enclosure.

Out of This World Rollercoasters

January–May 2024

I modelled rollercoaster motion under different planetary gravitational conditions, accounting for friction and air resistance. The project received the SciFest Maths in Science Award.

The MultiTasker — Modular Assistive Device

May 2021–July 2024

A customisable, 3D-printed tool for people with limited hand mobility, designed around each user’s needs. The project won three awards at regional and national levels.
